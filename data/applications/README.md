# Channel and backward-facing-step applications

The two NPZ snapshots are unchanged copies of saved finite-element point
samples. `provenance.json` records the source hashes, numerical-core hashes,
parameters, resolution, time step, snapshot time, section and color limits.
No interpolation in time or new PDE solve was performed for this revision.

- `channel_t2.0000.npz`: straight channel, K12, dt=0.005, t=2.
- `step_t0.7150.npz`: backward-facing step, K4, dt=0.005, t=0.715,
  step 143 (frame 144 when the initial state is included).
- `validation.json`: native-volume channel mesh/control comparisons and the
  completed K4 step trajectory audit, extracted from the existing reports.
- `step_time_gate.json`: the completed K4, T=2 comparison of dt=0.005 and
  dt=0.0025, using native volume L2 differences.

The step images are qualitative coarse-grid illustrations. They do not
constitute final fine-grid acceptance, a resolved reattachment-length study,
or evidence of a net magnetic pumping gain. Both geometries have prescribed
nonzero inflow after the ramp, and their inlet cross-sectional areas differ.

Figures are generated by `scripts/plot_applications.py`. Matplotlib draws
the native sample vectors, domain frame and color surface; a LaTeX/TikZ
wrapper typesets the colorbar labels in Latin Modern. Figure numbers and
captions are supplied only by the manuscript. The scalar surface interpolation
is for display and is not used for the numerical error or validation metrics.

All lengths used for glyph display are linear in field magnitude. The
magnetization in the step figure is sampled on y=0.1; its vectors retain
all three components. Other left panels use sparse volume samples. The
straight-channel right panels use z=0.1 and the step right panels use y=0.1.
These are deliberately different section orientations, explicitly identified
in the captions. The three fields of each configuration use one common
snapshot time.
